# my design guide

## colors
- main:          #2B4162   (headings, buttons)
- secondary:     #68D8F4   (accents, little details)
- light neutral: #F8FAFC   (background)
- dark neutral:  #282934   (text, borders)

## sizes
- heading:     4vh
- subheading:  3.25vh
- body:        2.5vh

## fonts
- headings: something bold
- body:     something calm and readable

## layout
- notebook pages
- tabs for different moments
- polaroids on each page w/ captions
- add button on top

## motion + sound
- sections fade up as they appear
- one soft click on the "keep going" button, with a mute toggle
- images hover
